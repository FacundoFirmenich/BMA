# Naming and lineage

## Prospective names

- **BayME** remains the general model and research architecture.
- **Bayesian Markets App (BMA)** is the physical-market application and the
  prospective Group 10 BIND product.
- Historical filenames, schemas, reports and freezes containing `BayME` remain
  unchanged. Their hashes and original jurisdiction take precedence over the
  new product label.

The rename is therefore a branch separation, not a claim that BayME began in
fish markets and not a retroactive transfer of evidence.

## Product boundary

BMA is not a fish predictor, a Mercabarna application or a router. It is a
market-learning system whose units of inference vary by venue:

- Galicia: species x first-sale auction x day;
- Andalucia: species x auction x layer x day;
- Mercabarna fruit/vegetables: product x origin or valid aggregation x day;
- Mercabarna flowers/plants: product x origin x day;
- future industrial markets: only after source, licence, completeness and
  identifiability gates pass.

The invariant is the controlled cycle and the local claim jurisdiction, not a
single universal likelihood or a single global score.

## BIND programme boundary

BMA is one of three products:

1. `{Kwancode}` — local operations orchestration and instruction authority;
2. KwanDocs — evidence and instruction governance;
3. BMA — Bayesian physical-market intelligence.

KCH is shared genealogy and control discipline. It does not allow one product
to inherit another product's evidence, permissions, support or execution.
