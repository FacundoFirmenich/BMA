# Evidence boundary

## Supported

- Historical physical-market campaigns and artifacts exist across Galicia,
  Andalucia and Mercabarna.
- Those campaigns demonstrate executable custody, freeze, adjudication and
  future-only learning mechanisms in bounded jurisdictions.
- Local favorable, neutral, adverse, out-of-support and non-estimable results
  all carry information and remain visible in `evidence/registry.json`.

## Not supported

- global superiority of BMA over baselines;
- transfer of a Galicia or Andalucia result to Mercabarna or an industrial
  venue;
- prospective validity of a retrospective replay;
- commercial or industrial outcome claims;
- automatic procurement or pricing authority;
- treating missing publication as economic zero;
- treating a stable HTTP response as stable bytes, a closed publication or an
  observed no-trade state;
- treating customs statistical value divided by mass as a winning bid, spot
  quote or firm-level transaction price;
- using `Z_post`, a model router or an aggregate metric as the economic
  posterior.

## Required claim key

Every performance or authority statement must be addressable by:

```text
product x market x node x horizon x regime x target x comparator x decision edge
```

If one coordinate is missing, the statement is a diagnostic summary rather
than an operational claim.

## Public-repository boundary

This repository includes source code, derived receipts and an evidence registry.
It does not publish raw market data. A source being publicly reachable does not
automatically establish redistribution rights or a stable source contract.
